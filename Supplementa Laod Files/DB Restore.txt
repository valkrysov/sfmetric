One small note on pooling

You're pointing at the direct host (ep-flat-rice-afl6klhi.c-2.us-west-2.aws.neon.tech), not the pooler host (ep-flat-rice-afl6klhi-pooler.c-2.us-west-2.aws.neon.tech) you were also given. For a Streamlit app with potentially multiple concurrent users each triggering their own pd.read_sql() calls, Neon's pooled connection is generally the better choice for the live app (similar reasoning to why we used Supabase's Session Pooler for scripts, but pooling matters even more here for concurrent web traffic). Consider using:

toml
DATABASE_URL = "postgresql://neondb_owner:YOUR_REAL_PASSWORD@ep-flat-rice-afl6klhi-pooler.c-2.us-west-2.aws.neon.tech/neondb?sslmode=require"
