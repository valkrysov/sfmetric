Practical rule of thumb going forward
After any large historical backfill (like this 1993-2005 addition) → re-run all three DataSF loaders once.
After routine monthly listing/sales updates → skip re-running them by default. Only re-run if you specifically know a meaningful batch of new construction or newly-created properties (not just newly-listed existing ones) came in.
Periodically as a safety net (say, quarterly) → re-run them anyway, just to catch any accumulated new properties from several months of small additions, without doing it every single cycle.
